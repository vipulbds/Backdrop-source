    ABOUT US

    At Backdropsource, we believe your visuals tell your story — and we’re here to help you tell it brilliantly. With over 21 years of global experience and thousands of happy customers, we’ve become a go-to name for photographers, event planners, marketers, and brands looking for premium-quality custom displays and backdrops.
