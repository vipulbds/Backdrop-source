Notes on the count

  • 15 systems, 24 files, about 22,000 lines. Two of those files are copies rather than new work — GEO-ENGINE-PASTE-THIS.gs is byte-identical to the GEO engine script, and EXEC-RAG-v2-PASTE-THIS.gs is a v2 revision of the RAG report. Counting only original work gives 22 files.
  • Four systems are built but not yet switched on — cross-border, visitor analytics and MoM comparison are all waiting on a Google Sheet ID being pasted into their CONFIG, and the GTM purchase pixel is written but not confirmed connected.
  • Two are deliberately retired — Canada OCT and the USA-only pixel were both generalised into the unified multi-country system. They're listed because the work exists, not because it's running.
  • Theme layout files (catheme.liquid, themeusa.liquid, uktheme.liquid) only carry one-line wiring into these systems, so they aren't counted as separate builds.
  • Not counted here at all: the sponsorship platform's Liquid pages, the PrintFabrix storefront work, and the ads landing pages — those are front-end builds, not automations.
