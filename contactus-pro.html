Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?
Most orders ship within 1–2 business days. Standard delivery is 3–7 business days; express options are available at checkout.
Can I request a custom size or colour?
Absolutely. We offer custom sizing and colour matching on bulk orders. Select "Custom Backdrop" above and share your brief.
Do you offer fabric samples?
Yes — swatches are available for our muslin and vinyl ranges. Reach out via the form and we'll arrange it for you.
What's your return policy?
We accept returns within 30 days on unused, unmodified items. Contact us with your order number to start the process.
Do you have wholesale pricing?
Yes. Studios, schools and resellers can apply for a wholesale account. Use the "Wholesale" tab above to get started.
Best backdrops for newborn photography?
Our soft muslin and wrinkle-resistant fabric backdrops are the go-to for newborn sessions — gentle on skin and easy to style.
